Records Catastro data you checked yourself: the 20-character cadastral reference of the flat, its built area and year, and
the **reference value** (valor de referencia) with the date you looked it up.

The reference value cannot be fetched automatically: the Sede Electrónica del Catastro only gives it after identification
(Cl@ve or certificate) to the owner, or to anyone by reference and date with a CAPTCHA, and its terms forbid automated
access. Look it up at https://www1.sedecatastro.gob.es/ and record it here. It matters because transfer tax (ITP) is paid on
the higher of price and reference value; the analysis uses it from then on.

Data given here is marked `Manual` and is never overwritten by the automatic Catastro lookup. Area and year feed the red flags
(advertised area far above the Catastro, building of 50 years or more without an IEE) and the renovation estimate.
